# Free IT Asset Inventory Template

A free, editable CSV template for tracking company laptops, monitors, peripherals and other equipment. Created as an educational resource by [Stackroom](https://stackroom.io/).

## Download

- [Download the inventory CSV](it-asset-inventory-template.csv)
- [View the resource page](https://stackroomio.github.io/it-asset-inventory-template/)

## Getting started

1. Download the CSV and open it in Excel, Google Sheets or another spreadsheet editor.
2. **Delete the two DEMO rows** and replace them with real asset records.
3. Give each asset a unique internal `Asset ID`; record a serial number where available.
4. Keep `Status`, `Assigned To`, `Location`, and `Last Verified Date` up to date.
5. Restrict access to inventory records containing employee or sensitive device information.

### Suggested status values

`Available`, `Assigned`, `Reserved`, `Maintenance`, `Pending Sanitisation`, `Retired`.

## What is included?

17 columns for identification, purchasing, location, assignment, status, warranty and inventory verification. The CSV is intentionally straightforward and contains **fictional demonstration data**.

## Important notes

This is a starter template, not a secure asset management system. CSV spreadsheets offer no built-in roles, authentication, audit history, or enforced workflows. Do not publish actual asset records, serial numbers, employee names or other internal inventory information in this public repository.
