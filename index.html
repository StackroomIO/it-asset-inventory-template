IT Asset Inventory Template

Download a simple CSV starter template for recording laptops, monitors, equipment assignments, statuses and warranty dates. Use it in Excel or Google Sheets.

Download CSV template

What's included?

  • Unique asset IDs, device details and serial numbers
  • Purchase date, cost and warranty expiry
  • Location, department and current assignment
  • Asset condition, lifecycle status and last verification date

How to use it

  1. Download and open the CSV.
  2. Remove the two clearly marked fictional demo rows.
  3. Enter your organisation's actual assets in a private copy.
  4. Keep location and assignment status updated after every handover.
  5. Reconcile records with a periodic physical inventory.

Suggested asset statuses

Available · Assigned · Reserved · Maintenance · Pending Sanitisation · Retired
